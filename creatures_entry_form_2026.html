Theme: A Gila "Creature" is a mammal, reptile, bird, fish, or insect that lives in the Gila River watershed. Entries may be any medium or style but must represent, or be inspired by, an actual (not mythical) Gila creature.

Eligibility: Artists must be 18+. Original work only — no AI or non-original images. Created within the past 24 months. Not previously shown locally or at GCAG Gallery. Juried show.

Commission: 25% on all sales. Max 2 entries per artist — one entry per form.
